$$
\frac{\eta}{\eta_0} = 1 + \kappa\,\frac{c^2}{v_s^2} + O\!\left(\frac{c^4}{v_s^4}\right),
$$

$$
\kappa = \frac{3 v_p^4 - 4 v_p^2 v_s^2 + 3 v_s^4}{4 v_p^2 \left(v_p^2 - v_s^2\right)}
$$

For Poisson's ratio 0 to 0.5, $\kappa$ lies between 0.72 and 0.87, and the Rayleigh speed $c_R$ (the root of the denominator of $\eta$) between 0.874 and 0.955 $v_s$.
