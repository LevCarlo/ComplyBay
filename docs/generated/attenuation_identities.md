$$
c(f) = \frac{\omega}{\operatorname{Re} k} = v_\mathrm{ref}\left(\frac{f}{f_\mathrm{ref}}\right)^{\gamma},\qquad \frac{\operatorname{Re} M}{-\operatorname{Im} M} = \cot(\pi\gamma) = Q,\qquad \operatorname{Im} k = \frac{\omega}{2 Q c}\left[1 + O(Q^{-2})\right]
$$

with $k = \omega/\tilde v$ and $M = \rho\tilde v^2$.
