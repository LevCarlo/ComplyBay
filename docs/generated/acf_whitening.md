$$
\text{power: } \Phi = \frac{A^2}{\max\!\left(\overline{A^2}_W,\, \ell \max_\mathrm{band}\overline{A^2}_W\right)},\qquad
\text{amplitude: } \Phi = \left(\frac{A}{\max\!\left(\overline{A}_W,\, \ell \max_\mathrm{band}\overline{A}_W\right)}\right)^{2}
$$

with $A = \lvert H\rvert$, $\overline{\,\cdot\,}_W$ the mean over $2N + 1$ frequency samples, $N = \mathrm{round}(W / 2\Delta f)$, and $\ell$ = `water_level`.
