$$
\boxed{\;\hat R = \sqrt{\frac{\frac{n-1}{n} W + \frac{B}{n}}{W}}\;},\qquad W = \frac{1}{2M} \sum_{j} s_j^2,\quad \frac{B}{n} = \operatorname{Var}_j(\bar x_j)
$$

with each of the $M$ chains cut into two halves $j$ of $n$ draws, $s_j^2$ their variances and $\bar x_j$ their means (both with $n - 1$ or $2M - 1$ in the denominator).
